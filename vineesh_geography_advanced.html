About me

Connecting geographical thinking with spatial technologies and data-driven research.

Focused on advancing knowledge and expertise in Geography, GIS, Remote Sensing, Geographical Data Science, and Analysis, I am deeply committed to teaching and conducting research in these fields. I am particularly interested in leveraging data-driven approaches for informed decision-making and crafting maps that support effective analysis and interpretation of spatial information.

Current academic focus

My current academic interests focus on applying deep learning and emerging computational technologies to geographical research and spatial data analysis.
